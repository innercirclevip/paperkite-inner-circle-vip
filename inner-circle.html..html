Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your Inner Circle membership.

How do I get access?

Request access by choosing your tier, then contact management to arrange payment and submit proof. Once approved, you'll receive login credentials.

Can I upgrade my membership?

Yes! Contact management to upgrade your tier at any time.

What happens when my membership expires?

You'll lose access to premium content but your account remains active. Renew anytime by contacting us.

How does the Platinum Tour access work?

Platinum members are automatically invited to join us in-person on the next tour — backstage, soundcheck, and all.
